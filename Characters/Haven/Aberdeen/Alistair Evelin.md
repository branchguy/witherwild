Beautiful, charming and playful, adored by all member of the fallen [[House of Evelin]]

#### Personality
- Has deep ambition to obtain what he wants: Fame, entertainment, women
- Will use his beauty and his social allure to hide his true goals, and often is so successful he crosses the barrier of the law often. 
- He has deep insecurity of losing others attention, and will do anything to gain it.
- Trusts few, but when trust is gained will use that character like a trustworthy tool


#### Relationships
- Would confide in [[Miron Evelin]], but never reciprocated
- Liked to fabricate subtle rivalries between [[Kaen Evelin]] and [[Caedric Evelin]]
- Always put on a show for his father [[Lord John-Paul Evelin]]
- Seeks subtle vengeance against [[Lord Severic Draemont]]

#### Current Status
- Age: 22
- Weapon: Hidden Knuckle Daggers
- Currently is living in [[Colossa]] as an actor for largest theater in the captial
