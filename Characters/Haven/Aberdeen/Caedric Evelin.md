The straight-laced former heir and first son of the [[House of Evelin]]
Son born from [[Lord John-Paul Evelin]]'s first arranged marriage, [[Lady Maria Evelin]] who supposedly died in childbirth years ago.
#### Personality
- Proud of the Evelin name and hence is obsessed with restoring honor to the House, but will never show emotion on his face
- Calculating chess fanatic, the world is his game and he must win
- Raised to be Lord, but politician at heart



#### Relationships
- Animosity towards [[Kaen Evelin]]
- Takes care of [[Alistair Evelin]]
- Looks down on [[Miron Evelin]]

#### Current Status
- Age 30ish
- Weapon: Great Axe
- Currently resides in [[Aqualith]] as a clerk working his way up to the courts
