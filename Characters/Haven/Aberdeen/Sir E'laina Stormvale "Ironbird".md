A seasoned warrior famed for a daring aerial rescue during the Ridge Campaign, earning the moniker **Ironbird**. Stern but fair, she mentored [[Kaen Evelin]] in discipline and tactical insight

Died in the coup trying to protect [[Kaen Evelin]] and gave him her weapon

- Steely Idealist: Believes chivalry can still thrive even in a corrupt capital, though cynicism nips at her heels.
- Mother-in-Arms: Shows affection through relentless drills and dry humor; a rare smile means more than words.
- Secret Guilt: Haunted by comrades she couldn’t save during the Ridge Campaign, fueling her drive to prepare [[Kaen Evelin]].



Weapon of Choice: Glaive / Spear
