Second son of the [[House of Evelin]] the first born from [[Lady Katherine Evelin]] 
#### Personality 
- Calm, thoughtful and quietly loyal member of the fallen [[House of Evelin]]. 
- Diplomatic and empathic to the point of reading rooms with unsettling accuracy
- Will sacrifice himself for others often
- He secretly doubts that the [[House of Evelin]] way of "gentle but unwavering" will ever prevail

#### Relationships
- Confidant to [[Kaen Evelin]]
- Loyal to [[Caedric Evelin]]'s cause, but skeptical
- Fed up with [[Alistair Evelin]]'s mischievous behavior

#### Current Status
- Age 27ish
- Weapon of Choice: Shortbow
- Currently resides on the outskirs of [[Fanewick]]
