A lithe, ferocious fighter with a scarred cheek and a cold, taunting smile. He resents [[Kaen Evelin]]'s noble blood but respects raw skill, and was the force that pushed [[Kaen Evelin]] to grow in the underground arenas of [[Colossa]]'s [[Blood Hollow]]

- Performative Predator: Loves the roar of the crowd; every fight is part duel, part theater.
- Hidden Code of Honor: Though vicious, he despises cheap shots and respects a worthy opponent—sometimes to his own detriment.
- Scarred Past: Whispers suggest he once served in Colossa’s city guard before a betrayal drove him underground.
