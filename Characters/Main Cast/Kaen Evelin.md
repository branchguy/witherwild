Fallen Nobility of [[Aberdeen]] of [[Haven]], son of the late Marquis [[Lord John-Paul Evelin]] and [[Lady Katherine Evelin]] of the [[House of Evelin]] that once ruled over [[Marr Domain]]. Both parents fell ill to the [[Serpent's Sickness]], but only Katherine succumbed to it. John-Paul was assassinated by a rivaling faction that took over [[Aberdeen]] led by [[Lord Severic Draemont]]

Has three brothers:
- [[Caedric Evelin]] - The eldest, was the former heir to the marquis title and resents [[Kaen Evelin]] for fall of the [[House of Evelin]]
- [[Miron Evelin]] - The second eldest, the melancholic  diplomat and empathic brother to [[Kaen Evelin]]
- [[Alistair Evelin]] - The youngest, playful, charming and adored by all brother with deep ambition and insecurity

> "Bad things happen to good people"

Had two companions before the coup:
- [[Vaelran "Bloodrend" Torvayne]] - [[Kaen Evelin]]'s rival during the underground fights in [[Colossa]] 
- [[Sir E'laina Stormvale "Ironbird"]] - [[Kaen Evelin]]'s fallen guardian / mentor that he learned everything he knows from

Weapon of Choice:
- A flexible Glaive / Spear that never breaks, but instead will bend to deflect blows, from his mentor

Glass Cannon Warrior
